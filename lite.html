self-balancing electric dirtbike
mechanical / power electronics / controls

Gas pit bike stripped to the bare frame, rebuilt electric, then handed to a controller that rides it with nobody on the seat.

  • QS165 mid-drive, chain to the stock rear sprocket, Fardriver ND72360 controller
  • 72V 20Ah pack built by hand from salvaged, capacity-matched 18650s, nickel strip spot welded
  • Arduino + BNO055 IMU, 110 Hz balance loop, countersteer to a target roll angle then correct on yaw error
  • Steering via linear actuator regeared 5:1 through an IBT2 H-bridge, failsafe cut on the throttle line
  • PPO policy pretrained in MuJoCo so it could fail in simulation before touching hardware
clusterleg
firmware reverse engineering / tooling

Tuning, flashing and custom firmware for Volkswagen Auto Group cars, tested on my own 2017 A3. Built on top of VW_Flash, where most of the credit goes.

  • Binary-patched the ESP32 CAN bridge firmware to reach the instrument cluster: dumped the image, retargeted an unused link's CAN IDs, re-fixed the checksum and SHA-256 so it still boots
  • Worked out VW's coding login order from a UDS 0x24 refusal, then built a long-coding tool with backup, readback verification and restore to stock
  • Desktop deck wrapping every action, resumable block flashing to recover a hung flash, dry run until confirmed
  • 32 offline tests against a simulated cluster
  • Tunes written by me

animatronic tail
product design / mechanism
DESIGNED, SOLD AND BUILT ON COMMISSION

Segmented tail that swings as a pendulum off a single 35kg servo, so the whole spine follows through on its own momentum with no motor per joint.

  • Printed in TPU, bearingless design using partial M3 rods instead of bearings
  • Segments taper toward the tip so the swing reads natural rather than uniform
  • Belt clip mount, no harness needed
  • Taken from prototype to a product people have paid for
smgprototype
cad / metal fabrication demonstration
NONFUNCTIONAL / DOES NOT FIRE

A CAD and fabrication demonstration piece. Does not fire and is not built to, the barrel is deliberately undersized. Designed as a complete assembly first so travel and clearance could be checked before anything was cut.

  • 4mm steel frame cut by hand and welded, aluminium brackets clamped on to hold it square through the heat
  • Aluminium slide with steel laminated between the layers at the wear and lockup points
  • Barrel finished by electrochemical machining, retained in collars
  • Every part my own design, worked out by studying teardowns and reverse-engineering how the mechanisms index
rcflame
rc mechanism / actuation

Combat robot chassis with a butane ignition payload driven off a rack and pinion that pushes the can forward into a larger nozzle to open it.

  • Ignition by nichrome wire run off a 40A ESC, sitting in the spray path
  • Feed on its own RC channel so it cannot fire from a drive input
  • Built on an antweight printed chassis running N20 gearmotor drives
